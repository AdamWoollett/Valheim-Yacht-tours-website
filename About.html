About Us

We are a group of Vikings who have explored vast amounts of the world of Valheim via Karve and Longship as we set out to free this land from the clutches of odin's foes.

We have decided that we could not keep all of these beautiful landscapes to ourselves, so we are offering tours around the world of Valheim to allow you to take in the natural beauty of this world as well.

Our time here has made us experts at sea-faring. We have honed our skills carrying many shipments of cargo between our settlements. We can navigate the waters of Valheim's oceans with ease.

Our team are veteran warriors who have vanquised many of the foes of Valheim. You are in safe hands when you take a tour with us.
