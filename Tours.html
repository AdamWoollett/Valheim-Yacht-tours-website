Around the Black Forest:

This tour goes around the borders of a Black Forest Biome. Marvel at the height of the Fir and Pine trees, along with the deer and birds that weave amongst them. Keep an eye out and you may even spot a troll lumbering amongst the trees.


Around the Mountain Ranges:

Want to marvel at stunning vistas that are almost as big as the world tree? Then come with us on a tour past the sea-side mountain ranges. These mountains are a wonder of Valheim and are not to be missed. While they may look desolate, they are teeming with wildlife. The howls of wolves often accompany our tours, and often tourers can spot roaming wolf packs and Drakes soaring above the mountain tops.


Around the Plains:

Tour the borders of the wide open plains. Witness the enormous stone obelisks that dot the plains whose origins are still unknown. Fuling camps can easily be spotted across the plains. Witness these creatures going about their daily lives from the safety of our vessels. We can also offer Plains tours that go in close vicinity to a Sealed Tower, which is overrun with Fulings.


Night tours:

All of our tours have the option to take place at night. The stillness of the night offers a unique perspective and allows us to witness the lives of the creatures on the shore at night. Roving packs of Fenrings can be seen on the mountains. Patrolling packs of Fulings lit by torchlight can be seen on the Plains. The experience of our tours under the light of the Moon, Stars and World Tree is not to be missed.
